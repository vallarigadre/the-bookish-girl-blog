---
title: Shipping and returns
description: How orders from The Bookish Girl shop are made, shipped and returned.
---

**Digital downloads** (trackers and printables) are delivered instantly after purchase through Beacons. Because they are digital files, they can't be returned, but if a file doesn't work for you, get in touch and I'll make it right.

**Bookish merch** is made to order just for you by my print partner, so it isn't sitting in a warehouse. Each item is printed after you order, then shipped. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as your order ships.

**Damaged or misprinted items:** if your order arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, email me a photo within 30 days of delivery and I'll send a replacement or a refund.

**Returns:** because every item is made to order, I can't accept returns for change of mind or the wrong size. Please check the size details on each product before you order.

**Payments** are handled securely by PayPal. You can pay with a PayPal account or a card. I never see or store your card details.

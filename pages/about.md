The Bookish Girl is a cozy reader's corner of the internet: romance by trope, mysteries and thrillers you can't put down, and gentle feel-good books about bookshops, cafes and second chances. Every list is built around what readers actually search for, with the Kindle Unlimited books clearly marked, so you can find your next favorite book fast.

## Who picks the books

Hi, I'm Vallari. I read every single day: mostly contemporary romance and romantasy, plus mysteries, thrillers and quiet, comforting books like Days at the Morisaki Bookshop when I want something different. These lists come from a reader, not a robot.

## What we promise

- **Real books, real details.** Every title, author and series order is checked before it goes on a list.
- **Kindle Unlimited, clearly marked.** Books move in and out of Kindle Unlimited often, so always check the Amazon page before you borrow.
- **Moods and tropes first.** Lists are organized by the moods and tropes you love, not by what is paying the most.
- **Open about affiliate links.** Some links earn a small commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it is always disclosed.

## New lists

New lists go up every Sunday and Wednesday.

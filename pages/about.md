The Bookish Girl is a romance reader's corner of the internet. Every list is built around the tropes readers actually search for, with the Kindle Unlimited books clearly marked, so you can find your next favorite book fast.

## Who picks the books

Hi, I'm Vallari. I read romance every single day, mostly contemporary romance, romantasy and the occasional thriller when I need a palate cleanser. These lists come from a reader who lives in the genre.

## What we promise

- **Real books, real details.** Every title, author and series order is checked before it goes on a list.
- **Kindle Unlimited, clearly marked.** Books move in and out of Kindle Unlimited often, so always check the Amazon page before you borrow.
- **Tropes first.** Lists are organized by the moods and tropes you love, not by what is paying the most.
- **Open about affiliate links.** Some links earn a small commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it is always disclosed.

## New lists

New lists go up every Sunday and Wednesday.

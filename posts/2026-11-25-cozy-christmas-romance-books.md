## Why Christmas romance hits different in December

Holiday romances work because the season does half the emotional work. Everyone is going home, old feelings are waiting by the tree, and a single snowstorm can strand two stubborn people together for days. You know there will be a happy ending, and in a month that can feel busy and loud, that promise is the coziest part.

I also love how many tropes fit inside a Christmas book. Fake dating at the family party, enemies decorating side by side, second chances with the one who got away: the holidays make every trope feel a little warmer.

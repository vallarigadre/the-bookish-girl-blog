## Why this trope never gets old

The best enemies to lovers books are really about being seen. Your rival is the one person who has been paying close attention to you the whole time, even if it was only to find the next thing to argue about. When the fighting finally stops, that attention turns into something tender, and that switch is what keeps us turning pages at 2 am.

It also gives you the best banter in romance. Two people who are equally stubborn and equally sharp make every scene crackle, and the first time one of them admits the other was right is better than any grand gesture. If you love the push and pull, look for books where the hate has a real reason behind it, an old hurt, a rivalry with stakes, a family feud, because the payoff feels so much bigger.

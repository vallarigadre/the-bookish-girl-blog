## A few things to know before you buy

**Older models are still around.** You may still see the 2024 Paperwhite and Colorsoft on Amazon. They are good Kindles, but the ones linked above are the newest models, so check the listing says 2026 if you want the latest.

**Ads or no ads.** Some Kindles come with "lockscreen ads," which means an ad shows on the sleep screen and a small banner on the home screen, not inside your books. You can pick a version without them when you buy, and the Signature Editions never have them.

**Audiobooks need Bluetooth.** Kindles have no speaker or headphone jack, so you listen to audiobooks with Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. Every Kindle in the current lineup supports Bluetooth.

- **Look for the Read for Free button** on a book's Amazon page. Titles move in and out of the program, so check before you borrow.

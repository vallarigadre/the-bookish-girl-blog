Some people want a love story full of fireworks. I want a man who grunts one-word answers at the whole town and then quietly fixes the heroine's porch step before she even notices it was broken. That is the magic of grumpy sunshine: the grump is soft for exactly one person, and the sunshine is the only one brave enough to find out.

## Why we can't get enough of grumpy sunshine

The best part of this trope is the reveal. For most of the book the grump is all crossed arms and short answers, and then you catch him doing something small and tender that only the sunshine gets to see. It feels like being let in on a secret, and that is incredibly satisfying to read.

It is also a lovely reminder that the sunshine character is not naive. Staying warm and hopeful after a hard life takes strength, and the best books in this trope show the grump realizing that. If you like your romance cozy with a little bite, this is the trope for you.

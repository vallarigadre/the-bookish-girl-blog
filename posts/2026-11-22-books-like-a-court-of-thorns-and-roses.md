## Which one should you read first?

- **You want the closest match to ACOTAR's world and heat:** Quicksilver.
- **You want fae court politics and a rival you love to hate:** The Cruel Prince.
- **You want the Beauty and the Beast start:** A Curse So Dark and Lonely, or Bonded by Thorns for adults.
- **You want a finished series to binge:** Kingdom of the Wicked, A Dawn of Onyx or A Touch of Darkness.
- **You want a slow burn:** A Court This Cruel and Lovely.
- **You want something gothic:** One Dark Window or Belladonna.

## What makes a book feel like ACOTAR

It is not only the fae. A Court of Thorns and Roses works because Feyre starts with nothing and grows into someone powerful, and the romance changes along with her. The world is beautiful and dangerous at the same time, and the love interest is not the obvious choice at first.

So when you are looking for your next read, look for a heroine who is underestimated, a magical world that does not want her there and a love interest with secrets of his own. Bonus points for a bargain or a bond that ties them together whether they like it or not. Every book on this list has at least two of those.

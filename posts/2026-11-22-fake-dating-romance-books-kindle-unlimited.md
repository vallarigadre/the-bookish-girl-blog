## Why we all fall for fake dating

Fake dating works because it gives two people permission. Permission to hold hands, to stand a little closer, to learn how the other one takes their coffee, all under the cover of "it's not real." The longer the act goes on, the harder it is to tell which touches are for the audience and which ones are not, and that blurry line is where the best scenes live.

It also builds in the sweetest kind of tension. Every fake dating book has a moment where one of them realizes the other has been paying attention for real: remembering a story from childhood, defending them in front of family, showing up when nobody is watching. That is my favorite page in any of these books, and these 13 deliver it.

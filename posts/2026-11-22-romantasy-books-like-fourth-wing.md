## What makes a book feel like Fourth Wing

It is not only the dragons. Fourth Wing works because the danger is real: people die, the rules are cruel and the heroine is underestimated by almost everyone. When a book gives you that pressure and then puts two people on opposite sides of it, the romance hits so much harder.

So when you are hunting for your next read, look for three things: a setting that wants your heroine dead, a love interest with secrets of his own and a bond, magical or otherwise, that ties them together whether they like it or not. Every book on this list has at least two of the three.
